Full description:
Temp Email is an Android app for creating a real temporary email address and receiving incoming messages without creating a user account or signing in with Google. When you open the app, a temporary mailbox is created through Mail.tm. You can copy or share the address, refresh the inbox automatically, read incoming messages, and delete messages when you are finished.

The app is designed for short-term and lawful uses such as testing signup flows, receiving verification emails where temporary addresses are allowed, and keeping one-time messages away from your primary inbox. Some websites may reject disposable email domains. Do not use temporary email for banking, healthcare, legal accounts, or any account that requires long-term access or future recovery.

Mailbox session data is stored locally using Android Keystore encryption. The app does not request location, contacts, or SMS permissions. Version 1.0.0 contains no ads, analytics SDK, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. The external mail service is Mail.tm and its own terms and policies apply.
