Privacy Policy — Temp Email

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how the “Temp Email” Android app handles data when you create and use a temporary mailbox.

1. What the app does

The app creates a real temporary email account through Mail.tm and lets you receive and read incoming messages. There is no Google sign-in and no user account inside the app.

2. Data handled by the app

  • The temporary email address that is created.
  • Mailbox session data required to access the inbox.
  • Incoming email messages and their metadata because they are processed by Mail.tm for delivery and display.

3. Local storage

Session data is stored locally on the device using Android Keystore encryption. The app does not request contacts, SMS, or location permissions.

4. Third-party mail service

The app uses Mail.tm to provide the temporary mailbox. Email and mailbox data are processed on Mail.tm systems under Mail.tm’s own terms and policies. The app does not claim that message content is inaccessible to the external mail provider.

5. Expiration and deletion

The app treats each mailbox as temporary for 60 minutes. When the timer expires, the app attempts to delete the account from Mail.tm and clears the local session. If network access is unavailable at expiration time, server-side deletion may not happen immediately.

6. Ads and analytics

Version 1.0.0 contains no in-app advertising, analytics SDK, Google sign-in, or in-app purchases. The external mail provider is Mail.tm as described above.

7. Security

The app connects to the mail service over HTTPS and stores the session token and mailbox password locally in encrypted form. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Responsible use

Use temporary email for lawful purposes and follow applicable laws and the terms of the websites and services where you use the temporary address. Do not use it for accounts that require long-term access or future recovery.

9. Contact

The developer’s official contact details must be added before final publication.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when new features or services are added. The last-updated date at the top will be changed accordingly.
